Khajani Welfare Society, Mathura

Founded in 2007 in Mathura, Uttar Pradesh, Khajani Welfare Society works across 152+ villages to empower women through vocational skill training, remedial education, GI-registered traditional crafts (Mathura Zari Poshak), Royal Sanjhi art, and sustainable grassroots livelihoods.
